How ClauseRadar works

Upload a PDF or DOCX, or paste your contract text, and choose a mode. ClauseRadar flags the risky clauses with the exact quote and a plain-English explanation, then Pro unlocks the safer rewrite and a ready-to-send counter-proposal email for every flagged clause.

Uncapped liability vs capped fees

Spot clauses that make you liable for losses far above your fee, and swap in a cap equal to the fees you were paid.

Pay-when-paid & Net-60 traps

Catch payment terms that leave you financing the client, and replace them with clear Net-14 terms and a late fee.

Pre-existing IP ownership clauses

Find wording that hands over your tools and past work, and keep ownership until the invoice is paid.

One-click counter-negotiation scripts

Get a polite, ready-to-send email with the safer clause included, for every risk we flag.

Frequently asked questions

Is this legal advice?

No. ClauseRadar is an automated AI review tool, not a law firm. It highlights common risks and suggests standard protective wording. For high-value or unusual contracts, have a qualified lawyer review the final terms.

How fast is the audit?

Most audits finish in a few seconds, and longer contracts (up to 10,000 characters) in well under 30 seconds.

Will asking for changes upset my client?

The counter-proposal emails are written to be polite and professional, presenting changes as standard protective terms. Many clients accept balanced terms, but there are no guarantees, and you decide what to send.

What does Pro include?

Pro unlocks every safer rewrite and counter-proposal email on every audit, plus unlimited Scope Guard checks, unlimited Payment Chaser invoices, PDF exports and use on up to 3 devices at the same time. Scanning and the risk score preview stay free.

How does billing work?

You pay by UPI and upload the payment screenshot. Each verified payment adds 30 days (monthly) or 365 days (yearly) of Pro to your account. Automatic renewal is not switched on yet, so you renew by paying again before your Pro time ends.

Is my contract stored?

By default, no. Scans are not saved in our database and we do not use your contracts to train models. If you choose Save to Contract Memory, we keep only the project terms and short clause excerpts you review and approve, never the full contract, and you can delete them any time. Contract text is processed by third-party AI providers under their API terms.

Can creators scan brand sponsorship contracts?

Yes. Choose BrandDeal Shield in the scanner and paste or upload your brand deal. ClauseRadar checks usage rights (organic versus paid ads and whitelisting, and how long), perpetual or irrevocable licences, category exclusivity, reshoot and edit limits, payment deadlines and kill fees, then writes a polite counter-email to the brand or talent agency.

Can job seekers scan offer letters and employment bonds?

Yes. Choose Job Offer and Bond in the scanner. ClauseRadar flags service bonds and training-cost penalties, 90-day notice period buyouts, withheld original certificates, side-project IP claims and non-competes, and drafts a polite clarification email to HR. For India it keeps Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act 1872 in mind. It is not legal advice, so ask a lawyer before you sign or fight a bond.

Does it work for Indian contracts?

Yes. Choose the India setting and the audit is tuned for the Indian Contract Act 1872 and MSMED Act payment norms. Choose Global for US, UK and remote contracts.
